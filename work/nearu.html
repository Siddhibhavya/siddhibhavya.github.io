Our research on how students discover, buy and sell on campus found gaps in trust, visibility and order management.

We studied discovery, trust, and the effort of running a small business alongside classes. We interviewed student entrepreneurs and buyers about margins, sourcing and commissioning peers, and looked at the platforms they already use.

Clear to browse, easy to manage between classes, and familiar enough to build trust between students.

Onboarding introduces NearU, verifies the student’s campus, and lets them choose to buy or sell, switchable at any time. It is a trusted, campus-only space, shaped to each user.

NearU is a campus commerce service:

  • Storefront for every student seller
  • Discovery across one college community
  • Order tools and verified buyers

Business model:

  • 1–2% fee on completed orders.
  • Seller boosts: paid promotion during fests and launches.
  • Campus partnerships: onboard sellers with colleges and fest teams.
  • Delivery commission: a small fee once delivery or pickup support starts.
  • Premium tools: analytics, storefront themes, priority visibility.
